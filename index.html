02 / principles

Calm systems beat clever demos.

01

Make the critical path explicit.

Payments, inventory, access, and decisions deserve deterministic rules and observable state.

02

Keep the evidence attached.

Useful automation shows its inputs, limits, fallback states, and audit trail.

03

Ship the smallest real thing.

A vertical slice that a person can use beats a wide plan that only sounds complete.
